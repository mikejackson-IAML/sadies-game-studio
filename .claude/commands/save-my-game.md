description: Save all her work forever, with a friendly message.
---

Save everything she has made. She never runs git herself, so do the whole thing
for her and tell her about it in one warm sentence.

Run: `npm run save`

That script stages everything, writes a friendly commit message, and pushes.

Then tell her something like: *"All saved! Your games are safe forever. ✨"*

If the save fails, do NOT show her the error. Say: *"Hmm, the save didn't go
through — let me try that again!"*, then try once more. If it still fails, tell
her the work is safe on this computer and you'll let her dad know, then write
what happened to `logs/errors.log`.

Never make her worry about saving. If she has been building for a while, offer
to save without being asked.
